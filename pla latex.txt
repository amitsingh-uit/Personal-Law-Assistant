\begin{frame}{Introduction}
\begin{itemize}
\item Personal Law Assistant is a web-based platform designed to provide general legal information and awareness to users.
\item It helps users understand basic legal concepts through articles, FAQs, and resources.
\item The system is intended for educational and informational purposes only.

\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Purpose of the Project}
\begin{itemize}
\item To create an easy-to-use legal information website.
\item To improve legal awareness among users.
\item To provide organized legal content online.
\item To reduce confusion related to basic legal concepts.
\item To make legal information easily accessible online.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Problem Statement}
\begin{itemize}
\item Legal information is often complex and difficult to understand.
\item Many people lack basic knowledge of laws and legal procedures.
\item Existing legal resources are scattered across multiple sources.
\item Common users face difficulty in finding correct legal information.
\item There is a need for a simple and user-friendly legal platform
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Conceptual Background}
\begin{itemize}
\item The system follows a web-based application architecture.
\item It uses frontend and backend technologies for development.
\item Users interact with the system through a web browser.
\item Backend handles data processing and content delivery.
\item Focus is on simplicity, clarity, and accessibility.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Technologies Used}
\begin{itemize}
\item Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
\item Backend: PHP / Node.js
\item UI Framework: Bootstrap
\item Web Browser: Google Chrome / Mozzila Firefox
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{System Architecture}
\begin{itemize}
\item User accesses the system using a web browser.
\item Requests are sent to the backend server.
\item Backend processes the request and fetches data.
\item Database stores legal articles and information.
\item Response is displayed to the user on the website.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Key Features}
\begin{itemize}
\item Legal articles and resources
\item Search functionality
\item FAQ section
\item Contact form
\item Responsive design
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Advantages}
\begin{itemize}
\item Easy access to legal information anytime.
\item Simple and user-friendly interface.
\item Saves time for users searching legal information.
\item Helps improve legal awareness.
\item Scalable and extendable system.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
\item Does not provide official legal advice.
\item Information depends on accuracy of content.
\item Limited to educational and informational use.
\item Cannot replace professional legal consultation.
\item Requires internet connectivity.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Future Scope}
\begin{itemize}
\item Chatbot-based legal assistance system.
\item Multi-language support for wider reach.
\item Integration with government legal portals.
\item Advanced search and filtering options.
\item Use of AI for legal guidance.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Conclusion}
\begin{itemize}
\item Personal Law Assistant is a useful legal information platform.
\item It helps users understand basic legal concepts easily.
\item The system improves legal awareness in society.
\item Simple design makes it accessible to all users.
\item With future upgrades, it has strong potential.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
